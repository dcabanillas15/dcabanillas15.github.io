Sustainability & civic engagement

Sustainability & Civic Engagement

Engineers shape the world we will live in. These workshops and actions have helped me understand the social and environmental responsibilities that come with this role.

ENSEEIHT · Year 1

The Trial of King Kong

In my first year at ENSEEIHT, I joined the audience of “The Trial of King Kong”, an interactive play in which spectators act as the jury of a fictional trial. The performance confronted us with gender-based and sexual violence, the mechanisms that allow it and the lasting damage it leaves on victims.

  • Gender-based violence
  • Prevention
ENSEEIHT · Year 1

Climate Fresk

At the very beginning of my engineering studies, I took part in a Climate Fresk workshop with other ENSEEIHT students. Piecing together the causes and consequences of climate change gave me a clear, science-based view of the crisis, and of the role engineers will have to play in responding to it.

  • Climate
  • Collective intelligence
Workshop

Planetary Boundaries Fresk

As a follow-up, I attended a fresk on planetary boundaries. It goes beyond climate to cover all the limits that keep the Earth habitable, such as biodiversity, freshwater and the nitrogen cycle, and shows how closely these issues are linked.

  • Biodiversity
  • Systems thinking
Toulouse universities

“Futur Proche” Festival

Through ENSEEIHT and the Toulouse universities, I attended the Futur Proche festival, a programme of talks, workshops and presentations on the climate transition. What I valued most was its scientific angle: researchers and innovators presented solutions already in use alongside technologies still being developed.

  • Science & innovation
  • Transition
ENSEEIHT · Start of 2026–27

ENSEEIHT Cleanwalk

At the start of this academic year, I took part in the ENSEEIHT cleanwalk, picking up litter in the streets around the school with other students. A small, concrete action that improves our shared environment and builds a sense of community.

  • Local action
  • Community
In my projects

Transition in practice

These issues also shape my technical work. My Prépa T² project studied thermal storage of renewable energy and the risk of rebound effects, and my internship report analyses how heat simulation supports energy efficiency and the study of urban heat islands.

See my projects

What it means for me as a future engineer

These experiences have convinced me that technical skill is not enough. An engineer must also measure the impact of what they build on people and on the planet, avoid technological solutionism, and help create a respectful and inclusive working environment. I want to bring these values into every project I take part in.
