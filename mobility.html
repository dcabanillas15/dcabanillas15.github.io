Future mobility

Three destinations, one goal

My aim is to apply computer science to scientific problems. Each destination below offers a different way to grow in that direction, in a country I feel drawn to.

Norway

NTNU, Trondheim

Why NTNU

NTNU is Norway's leading university of science and technology, with strong teaching in computer science, AI and scientific computing.

My project

Deepen numerical simulation and high-performance computing, in direct continuity with my Monte Carlo internship. I would also discover how a country so committed to the energy transition uses digital tools to manage it.

Beyond the classroom

Learning to live in a Nordic culture, and climbing on the granite walls of the Lofoten islands.

  • Scientific computing
  • AI
  • Simulation

Canada

Polytechnique Montréal, Québec

Why Montréal

Montréal is one of the world's major hubs for artificial intelligence, home to the Mila research institute. Polytechnique Montréal is a long-standing partner of French engineering schools.

My project

Specialise in AI and data science within a North American engineering school. Montréal would let me work in both French and English, an ideal setting to make my English fully professional.

Beyond the classroom

Discovering North American work culture and the climbing of the Laurentians.

  • Artificial intelligence
  • Data science
  • Bilingual setting

Chile

Universidad de Chile / PUC, Santiago

Why Chile

The Atacama Desert hosts some of the world's largest astronomical observatories, which generate massive amounts of data. This makes Chile a remarkable place for data science and large-scale computing.

My project

Work on scientific data processing in a Spanish-speaking environment, where my native Spanish lets me be fully operational from day one, both academically and professionally.

Beyond the classroom

Connecting with my Hispanic roots in Latin America, and exploring the mountains of the Andes and Patagonia.

  • Data science
  • Astro-informatics
  • Native Spanish
