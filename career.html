Research intern: Monte Carlo methods for heat transfer

LAPLACE laboratory (CNRS, Toulouse INP, Université Toulouse III) · GREPHE research group

Supervised by Stéphane Blanco and Richard Fournier, I worked on Stardis, a heat-transfer simulation software that uses Monte Carlo methods and ray tracing, developed by the company Méso-Star.

  • Learned the physics behind Monte Carlo methods, the C language and a Linux research environment.
  • Modified the solver so that thermal paths can travel by conduction inside a fluid, a first step towards modelling snow and water vapour.
  • Debugged the prototype by visualising thermal paths in ParaView.
  • Rewrote a practical exercise for beginners and wrote advice for future newcomers to the code.

Key takeaway: this internship confirmed my choice of the Digital Sciences department. I realised I enjoy working on large code bases, and that computer science opens the door to many scientific fields.
